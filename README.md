FloatChat is making ocean data accessible and actionable. It’s key to understanding our climate and protecting our planet.What if you could ask the ocean a question and get an instant answer? Meet FloatChat! This amazing AI platform uses data from thousands of robotic floats—called ARGO floats—all over the world. It’s making complex ocean science simple for everyone.Spotting Trends - Show Trend Chart)

FloatChat's job is to track the ocean’s health. It immediately shows you the Ocean Data Trends, like how water temperature and salinity change all year. See the pattern? Warmest in August, coldest in February. If anything looks unusual, like a sudden drop or spike, scientists instantly know something’s up.Tracking the Robots - Show Float Pop-up)
